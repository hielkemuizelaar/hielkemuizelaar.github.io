By train & on foot

Travel to Leiden Centraal. Allow approximately 15–20 minutes on foot to reach Rapenburg 73, depending on your route and walking pace. Leave the station on the city-centre side and follow the walking directions linked above.

Plan your train journey with NS, or use 9292 for public transport connections. Check the journey planner shortly before departure for up-to-date services.

By car

Parking in Leiden’s historic centre is limited. Leiden University lists Haagweg Parking as an option for visits to city-centre buildings. Check the operator’s current rates and transport options before travelling.

See Leiden University’s venue page for parking and building information.

Accessibility

The university lists accessible toilets and a lift accessed through an entrance opposite the main entrance. The main entrance has a threshold of approximately 4 cm and a manually operated door; some floors are accessible only by stairs. An accessible parking space is listed opposite Rapenburg 46, across the canal.

Because access depends on the workshop room, please check the university’s detailed accessibility information.
