Keynote speakers

  • Sanne Schuite-KoopsAssistant Professor · UMCG · Mental healthcare for psychosis

    Sanne Schuite-Koops is an Assistant Professor whose research focuses on improving mental healthcare for people with psychosis. Her current work applies AI and natural language processing to clinical practice, with a particular focus on speech-based biomarkers for diagnosing psychosis and predicting relapse.

    She contributes to TRUSTING, a Horizon Europe project developing a speech-based tool for use in everyday clinical care.

  • Kaiko.aiClinical AI for healthcare teams

    Kaiko.ai is a European clinical AI company that builds AI assistants for healthcare professionals. It develops its own medical foundation models and has open-sourced models for pathology and radiology CT.

    Its clinical AI workspace connects to hospitals’ existing systems. It helps clinicians reason across patient records and find the information they need, with the aim of lowering their cognitive workload while they stay in charge of clinical decisions. Kaiko started in oncology and has long worked with the Netherlands Cancer Institute (NKI), but it is now applying its technology to wider clinical uses.
