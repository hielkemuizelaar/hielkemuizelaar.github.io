CLINICAL NLP · LEIDEN 2027

Abstract submission

We welcome contributions on natural language processing in healthcare.

Topics

We invite abstracts on topics including, but not limited to:

  • Information extraction and text mining from clinical notes, reports and other health records
  • Large language models and generative AI in healthcare
  • Clinical decision support and applications in clinical practice
  • Evaluation, validation and benchmarking of clinical NLP systems

Work in progress and preliminary results are welcome.

Presentation

Accepted abstracts will be presented as an oral presentation or a poster during the workshop on 5 February 2027 in Leiden.

Deadlines

Abstracts must be submitted by Tuesday, 22 December 2026. Authors will be notified of acceptance on Friday, 8 January 2027.
